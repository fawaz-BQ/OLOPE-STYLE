How ordering works

Four simple steps from first message to finished outfit.

  1. Design it here

    Pick your style, material and colour above, then send it to us on WhatsApp.

  2. Share measurements

    Send your measurements or visit us to be measured, and agree on the style and price.

  3. We sew

    Your outfit is cut and sewn with careful attention to detail, with updates along the way.

  4. Fit and collect

    Try it on, request any final adjustments, and collect it ready to wear.
